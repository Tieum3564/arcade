Neon Invaders

Defend the sector through increasingly dangerous invasion waves and a boss battle every five levels.

Instructions

Destroy every invader before the formation reaches the ground line – each invasion or collision with an invader costs a life. Asteroids now emerge from the distance and grow as they approach. Move in four directions to dodge them and defeat the Dreadnought boss every fifth level.

Power-ups: R Rapid fire · S Spread shot · D Shield · + Extra life

Flight: moving forward increases your closing speed with incoming obstacles; moving backward reduces it. Distant asteroids cannot hit you until they reach your flight plane.

Scoring: keep destroying enemies to build your combo multiplier. Bosses award a large score bonus.

iPhone/iPad/Android: landscape is recommended. Use the virtual joystick and FIRE button. Drag the stick in any direction for smooth 360° movement, and fire simultaneously. Defensive barriers absorb both enemy and player shots.
